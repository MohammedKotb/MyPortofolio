A developer who starts with your business, not the code

I'm a software engineer specialising in Full-Stack .NET development. Most of my work is business software — the systems a company runs on every day — so I spend as much time on requirements as I do in the editor.

I started out in 2019 building freelance web projects: an e-commerce store, an e-learning platform, a management system for a training centre. Each one began with the same question — what does this business actually need to do? That habit stuck, and it's still how I open every project.

Since then I've worked as a Full-Stack .NET developer on internal systems at scale: asset management, ticketing, inventory, document management, HR, and CMS platforms. On those projects I analyse requirements with the business, design the solution across the API and database, build the Angular interface, then keep extending the product as new needs arrive.

I work well inside a team — pairing on problems, reviewing approaches, and planning features together — and just as comfortably as the single developer a client talks to directly. If you have a system to build, extend, or fix, I can take it from the requirements conversation through to something your users can actually rely on.

Internal business system
